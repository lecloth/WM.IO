1 · The hallway

The real walk at phone size. The grid folds into the hallway and it dresses up as you walk; past the way in the camera moves whenever you scroll and only then, straight, never turning. Each drawing hangs on its wall by a hinge at its near edge, swings out 40° as you approach and back as you pass, one hairline frame round it, its frosted label right under it, as wide as it, turning with it. Each canvas shows one of our made-up products. Play runs the way in; Walk moves through it all.

2 · The shut door, with the lock

The hallway’s end: a wall facing you with a door in it, in the hallway’s hairlines. The door stays shut. Its lock, the real “Cutaway Lock” (zbzzn, Printables 250197, CC BY 4.0), sits in it at eye level, face to you, big enough to read as a lock from the far end. Walk from the last drawing up to it: the walk ends with the lock filling the screen, the frame page 3 (into the lock) starts from.
