Asset sheet · page 2 · the hallway

1 · The hallway

The real walk at phone size (hallway pass 1). The hallway stretches out as a white sketch, the page’s grid drawn into depth (a dark sketch in dark mode); it dresses up and goes dark; the spotlights and the ceiling’s light strip come on; the frames appear, lit. Then scrolling is walking, at one steady pace, straight down the middle. Five frames, about a third of the hall’s height, at about 4, 7, 10, 13 and 16 along it, left, right, left, right, left: each hangs on its wall by a hinge at its near edge, swings out fully (90°, square to you) as you reach it, and folds back as you pass. Hallway pass 2: each is a black float frame (a thin moulding with depth, its top edge catching the light, the blueprint floating inside it with a dark shadow gap, a soft shadow on the wall) lit in its own spotlight, with a small light plaque under it exactly as wide as the frame (the title and the FIG line); the open frame’s story line shows in a dark caption band at the bottom (pass 3: the title only on the plaque, the plaques light paper). Pass 3: the camera is pre-ducked, one fixed eye height for the whole walk so each open frame with its plaque sits centred between the strip and the band; it never moves up or down, and on a portrait screen, through a narrower lens, it slides gently sideways so the frame is centred at about two thirds of the width. Frame 5 dresses from its blueprint into the real phones (local only), turning slowly. The ceiling’s strip is a soft line. All light and shine is painted into the surfaces once. Play runs the sketch, the dressing and the lights; Walk moves through it all.

1b · The five drawings

Hallway pass 2: the five blueprints in walking order, flat. 1 Where customers give up (the property app: the path as it is, they leave at the form; fixed, one tap and it’s sent) · 2 Design that understands people (the booking app in five layers, numbered from the bottom; the palette dots solid, faint, outline) · 3 Everything in one place (WhatsApp, Excel, paper and the register feed one database of joined tables) · 4 Speed (the same page before and after; no numbers) · 5 On every phone they carry (the booking app on the iPhone and the Android, as a blueprint; in the hallway it dresses into the real phones). Pass 3: no stock icons (no tick, smiley, person, phone glyph, pill, spinner or browser dots). Every word in them is a draft.

2 · The shut door, with its lock

The hallway’s end (pass 1): a wall facing you with the door centred in it (about 0.9 × 2.1), two panels, shut. Its lock (pass 2): the real “Cutaway Lock” (zbzzn, CC BY 4.0) in polished brass, the site’s one warm tone, lit by the studio light, on the door’s right edge strip between the panels, at handle height. Walk from the last frame up to it: the walk ends standing in front of the shut door. The approach to the keyhole is designed later; for now the screen turns blue and page 3’s first frame (the real “Cutaway Lock”, zbzzn, CC BY 4.0, in X-ray) fades in.
