# Sheet 03 Disciplines: builder D1 notes (slice 6)

Parts: `sheet-03` (websites, apps and phones, platforms and web apps, engineering and infrastructure), then D2's
`sheet-03b` (security, AI), then `sheet-03c` (consulting, monthly care). Six `.chapter` sections, all `data-sheet="03"`.

## What I built

- **One sticky scene per discipline**, the sheet 01 language:
  1. reading moment 1: the ancestor plate (full patent sheet, ink multiplied on paper) + a caption card;
  2. the figure lifts off the plate, travels to the board and redraws itself line by line (ink wiped left to right
     into potrace hairlines, which un-draw while today's drawing draws on behind the sweep);
  3. reading moment 2: the discipline's line, what we offer (mono tags), beside the finished, labelled drawing.
- **A years dimension line** under every drawing (plate year to "Today", value "163 years" etc.), the opening's
  dimension language. The line runs out with the sweep; its value shows only once fully measured.
- **Today's drawings** (code, hairlines, `site/js/sheet03/drawings.js`), no logos, no brands:
  - websites: a page pulled apart into five isometric layers (page, grid, content, shop and booking, hosting);
  - apps: today's phone at 1:1 (74 × 154 mm, sheet 01's outline) with screens, offline store and sync;
  - platforms: six services wired on two buses to one hub (the switchboard, today) inside a closed boundary with one
    locked door;
  - infrastructure: users → two load balancers → two zones of servers (fully cross-connected) → primary + replica →
    off-site backups;
  - consulting: a triangulation survey of a business from one baseline, angles computed from the drawing itself, north
    arrow and the sun it was found by (Holmes's solar theodolite);
  - monthly care (no ancestor): the drawing set's revisions table, Rev A → F appearing one per step, labelled
    "Example log · drawn to show the method".
- **Apps and phones is a compact recap**, not a repeat: Bell's Fig. 1 (sheet 01's traced data and webp, reused
  read-only) redraws in one sweep straight into today's phone; the caption says "Drawn on sheet 01" with a drawing
  detail-callout bubble link "Back to sheet 01" (`engine.targetOf('#sheet-01')`, keyboard focusable, focus ring).
- **Incoming chapter change into 03** (`site/js/sheet03/entry.js`, `MeasureEntry`): over the pinned sheet 02, two
  hairline dimension lines run out from a centre mark with arrowheads and live mono values (mm at 1:1:
  372.5 × 221.2 mm at 1440 × 900), extension lines drop, the frame is traced from opposite corners, the sheet appears
  inside the measured frame at p 0.62 (toggle), the label "Sheet 03 · Disciplines · Scale 1:1" sits under the width.
  Values never move (only digits change), on paper tabs. Dims clear at p 0.9, then the plate shows.
- **Between disciplines: the plan chest** (`ChestEntry`): the next sheet is drawn up from below, opaque, with a
  hairline leading edge, a second drawer-front hairline and a drawer tab ("Drawer 03.2 · Apps and phones") riding
  its edge. Used for all five later scenes, including consulting after D2's AI scene.
- **Freeze-frame:** every text element toggles (`.is-on`, ≤ .25 s fades). All my text (cards, labels, the span value,
  the care log) is off at p = units − 0.06, before the next sheet's entry passes over.
- **Reduced motion:** each discipline is two finished panels (plate + caption; drawing + labels + line), care is one
  panel. Apps shows Bell's Fig. 1 as a still. No scrubbing.
- **Mobile 390 × 844:** plate/drawing in the top ~45 %, reading cards at the bottom (sheet 01's mobile pattern),
  label sub-lines hidden, text ≥ 16 px. `scrollWidth === innerWidth` on mobile for my sections in both modes
  (the page as a whole is 417 px wide in reduced mobile because of D2's `s3b-lock` / `lk-*` SVG, not mine).
- **Checks:** desktop 1440 × 900 and 1920 × 1080, mobile 390 × 844, reduced motion, no console errors.
  `tools/d1/fps03.py` (wheel through all of 03): 59.5 fps desktop, 59.6 fps mobile, p95 16.8 ms, 0.3–0.4 % slow.

## Facts on the site and their sources

- "William Bullock's printing press, patented on 14 April 1863. Paper ran in from a continuous roll, was cut into sheets
  and printed on both sides in one pass, with nobody feeding it by hand. It was built for newspapers." Patent text
  (above); "for printing newspapers": historyofinformation.com/detail.php?id=5142, Britannica "William Bullock". I do
  not say "first": sources disagree on who and when (Britannica dates the roll-fed rotary to 1865; others cite Auer 1858).
- Firman caption: patent text (above). Secondary check: Scribner US 632,067 (1899) describes 252,576 as boards "to each
  of which the same telephone-lines are connected".
- Edison caption: patent text (above). I do not claim he was first (Hopkinson also patented a three-wire system).
- Holmes caption: "true north" = the patent's "true meridian"; time and latitude: patent text.
- "Nine drawings took it from his 1877 box to the phone in your pocket": sheet 01's history has Figs. 1–9 (ERAS).
- Years on the span lines: 2026 minus the patent year (163, 149, 144, 143, 143).
- Phone dims 74.0 × 154.0 mm: the same generic outline as sheet 01's today's phone (not a real product's size claim).
- Survey angles on the consulting drawing are computed from the drawing's own geometry (honest annotation, not data).
- No numbers, clients, certifications or people anywhere. The care log is labelled as an example.

## Decisions I made alone (with why)

- **Plates:** Bullock over Hoe (perspective Fig. 1 parallels Bell's Fig. 1; web-fed press = always on); Holmes's
  *solar theodolite* (measures by the sun before anything is built: a neat echo, and literally a theodolite).
- **Plates shown in ink on paper, not blue**: same as Bell's plate in sheet 01 (blue is the cyanotype reward there).
- **Numbering** 03.1–03.4 (mine), 03.5/03.6 assumed for D2 (security, AI), 03.7 consulting, 03.8 care.
- **Short title-block titles** ("Disciplines · Apps", "· Platforms", "· Infrastructure", "· Care") because long ones
  widen the fixed title block over the drawings' years line.
- **Title block by discipline:** Websites D/E/S (Design, Engineering, Strategy), Apps D/Sec/E, Platforms E/Sec/D,
  Infrastructure E/Sec/Strategy, Consulting Strategy/E/D, Care E/Sec/Strategy.
- **Units:** 6 per plate scene, 5 for apps (recap) and care. Timeline (p in viewport heights): plate reading 0.97–2.3,
  lift + travel 2.3–2.8, sweep 2.8–3.85, line reading 3.95–5.94 (apps: 0.97–2.1, sweep 2.15–3.1, read 3.2–4.94).
- The plan chest tab and the measure dims are the only text that moves, and only as part of a whole sheet passing /
  digits changing in place.
